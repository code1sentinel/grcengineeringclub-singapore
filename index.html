Definition

What GRC Engineering is

GRC Engineering applies engineering and systems thinking to governance, risk, and compliance, so that controls are built into systems and assurance is produced continuously by those systems.

Vision

Where we are headed

A community where GRC practitioners learn and build, so that they run GRC that is automated, continuous, and verifiable.

Our chapter values

Who belongs here

We are building an inclusive community. Bring a connection to Singapore, curiosity about the work, and a willingness to help others grow. That is enough to get started.

  • 01

    Singapore Connection

    You live, work, or study in Singapore, or you work on Singapore regulatory obligations from elsewhere in the region.

  • 02

    Curiosity First

    Experience is not required. If you are interested in GRC, cybersecurity, privacy, audit, risk, or the technology behind them, you belong here.

  • 03

    Safe to Contribute

    You never bring your employer's implementations, data, or internal documents into chapter work. We build on public framework text, so everyone can take part without putting their organisation at risk.

  • 04

    We Build

    You come ready to make something. Your first build can be a single control mapping. We learn by building and we share what we learn.

  • 05

    Respect + Repair

    You respect every member, engage across differences in good faith, and work through disagreement constructively.

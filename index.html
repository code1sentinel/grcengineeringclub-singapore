Definition

What GRC Engineering is

GRC Engineering applies engineering and systems thinking to governance, risk, and compliance, so that controls are built into systems and assurance is produced continuously by those systems.

Vision

Where we are headed

A community where every GRC practitioner in Singapore can learn and build, so that controls ship automated, continuous, and evidence-backed.

Our chapter values

Who belongs here

We are building an inclusive community. Bring a connection to Singapore, curiosity about the work, and a willingness to help others grow. That is enough to get started.

  • 01

    Singapore Connection

    You live, work, or study in Singapore, or you work on Singapore regulatory obligations from elsewhere in the region.

  • 02

    Curiosity First

    Experience is not required. If you are interested in GRC, cybersecurity, privacy, audit, risk, or the technology behind them, you belong here.

  • 03

    Safe to Contribute

    You never bring your employer's implementations, data, or internal documents into chapter work. We build on public framework text, so everyone can take part without putting their organisation at risk.

  • 04

    We Build

    You come ready to make something. Your first build can be a single control mapping. We learn by building and we share what we learn.

  • 05

    Respect + Repair

    You respect every member, engage across differences in good faith, and work through disagreement constructively.

Meeting topics that work

What we run

Every topic maps to Academy content or club themes, so members can go deeper after the meeting. Submit whatever you run through the Events tab.

Build sessions

Hands-on beats lectures. Pick one, bring laptops, ship something before the room empties.

  • Automate one control end to end

    Pick a SOC 2 or ISO control and build the evidence collection for it live.

  • Policy as code night

    OPA/Rego or Sentinel. Leave with a working policy gate in a pipeline.

  • Compliance as code with Terraform

    Guardrails, tagging standards, and drift detection as infrastructure.

  • Build an evidence pipeline

    API pulls to storage to tickets. The anti-screenshot workflow.

  • OSCAL workshop

    Catalogs and SSPs as structured data instead of Word documents.

CGE-P study nights

Run a study cohort for the Certified GRC Engineer – Practitioner. One domain per meeting, member-led.

  • Domain deep dive

    A member presents one CGE-P domain, the room works practice questions together.

  • Lab walkthrough

    Work an Academy hands-on lab as a group and compare approaches.

  • Exam readiness check

    Timed practice rounds. 60 questions, 72% to pass — train like it counts.

CGE-AUD sessions

The Auditor specialty gives audit-side members a track of their own.

  • Auditing engineered controls

    What evidence quality means when the control is a pipeline, not a policy PDF.

  • Automation for auditors

    Sampling, tie-outs, and workpapers with scripts instead of spreadsheets.

Career and community

Not every meeting needs a terminal open.

  • Breaking into GRC engineering

    Panel or AMA with members who made the jump. Big draw for first-timers.

  • Member wins and demos

    Lightning rounds: what members shipped at work this quarter.

  • Watch party plus discussion

    Screen a club YouTube episode or Patreon exclusive, then argue about it productively.
